## 2. Phase A + B Execution: Primary Dragnet $\to$ `180-Second First-Person Wall-Walk`

### Step A: Wide Primary Dragnet (The LLM Superpower)
Before brainstorming, inspect primary sources (never marketing blurbs):
- **Sponsor Changelog (<90 days):** Newly shipped hooks, precompiles, APIs, permissions, or edge models.
- **Open Maintainer Pain:** Sponsor GitHub Issues (`is:issue is:open sort:comments-desc`), Discord `#help` threads, SDK bug threads.
- **Insider Complaints:** Reddit / HN / X searches pairing the domain with `"workaround"`, `"manually"`, `"spreadsheet"`, `"impossible"`, `"annoying"`, `"broken"`.

### Step B: The 180-Second First-Person Wall-Walk (The Human Simulation)
Drop 30,000-foot summaries. Walk second-by-second through what an actual actor experiences at **the exact moment of friction**, capturing all **5 Informational Signatures**:

```markdown
1. EXACT SECOND IT BREAKS: What is on the screen, in their hands, or on the phone at t = 0..180s?
2. WHAT THEY TRIED FIRST: Why the standard tool (ChatGPT, Stripe, Metamask, Excel) stopped 10 lines short.
3. HARD MEASURABLE COST: Exact dollars leaked, minutes wasted, bps lost, or % dropped.
4. THE HATED WORKAROUND: The ugly copy-paste script, paper binder, or second tab they despise but still run daily.
5. THE "THIS IS INSANE" TRIGGER: Which Wall (`Operational Pain`, `Absurdity`, or `Dead-Screen Boredom`) snaps cleanly when we strike it?
```

## 3. Orthogonal 3-Channel Synthesis (5 Lobes × Bisociation × First Principles × SCAMPER)

Always generate **3 orthogonal candidates** across different creative mechanisms—never 3 variations of the same tool:

### Cognitive Filters Applied to Every Candidate (`5 Cerebral Lobes` — Ninja Nerd)
1. **Frontal Lobe (`Prefrontal + Broca`):** Inhibit the Hour-1/Hour-2 obvious crowd idea (**Giovanni Corazza Long Thinking**: discard your first 3 consensus ideas!). Compress the core into a **5-word Broca hook**.
2. **Parietal Lobe (`Stereognosis`):** What physical object, voice/QR/NFC tap, `/rob` button, or tactile control does the judge touch in 10 seconds?
3. **Temporal Lobe (`Wernicke + Hippocampus`):** Does this solve **what the sponsor actually needs to prove**, backed by graph memory of 1,164 historical builds?
4. **Occipital Lobe (`Visual Contrast`):** Is the visual delta (A/B diff, red `500` $\to$ green `422`, shader transformation, live refusal) obvious in **3 seconds**?
5. **Insular Lobe (`Insula — Taste & Empathy`):** Does it trigger genuine **wonder, relief, amusement, or indignation** instead of dead AI slop?

### Generative Engines
- **Dr. Ken Bloemer Bisociation (`Matrix 1 × Matrix 2`):** Collide the Sponsor Stack (`Matrix 1`) with an unrelated proven physical/industrial mechanism (`Matrix 2`: *56k dial-up modem*, *1968 Aviation TCAS*, *DTCC Netting*, *Neapolitan suspended coffee*, *Airport Captive Portal*).
- **Elon Musk First Principles:** Boil the sponsor's stack down to its raw byte, energy, fee, or cryptographic limit (e.g., *EIP-4844 128KB blob sizing* $\to$ `μBlob`; *pill optical dissolution curve* $\to$ `Peel`; *net spread after fees* $\to$ `Deltr`).
- **Jerry Lorenzo Restraint:** Solve an honest lived void and strip away secondary bloat until one unforgettable silhouette remains.

### The 7 SCAMPER Sharpeners
- **S — Substitute:** Replace $10k sensors or trusted middlemen with cheap webcams/ZK proofs (`OmNom`, `ZKP2P`, `SPC Wallet`, `BrailleBot`).
- **C — Combine:** Fuse 2–3 sponsor primitives into one indivisible state machine (`Incorpr`: NFT + MPC + TEE; `Meshmap`: AR laser tag + 3D LiDAR mapping).
- **A — Adapt:** Port a $10B mechanism (`Terraform` $\to$ `Txtx`, `Kubernetes` $\to$ `Zubernetes`, `Bazel` $\to$ `takegraph`, `Mirror Box` $\to$ `RECOVR`).
- **M — Modify / Magnify / Minify:** Shrink ML into `<256KB SRAM` (`TinyML Forest Fire`), microsecond risk gates (`Deltr`), or magnify hidden leaks (`Silicr` Leak Inspector).
- **P — Put to Another Use:** Repurpose image diffusion for scarce satellite training (`ShipSense`), VR racing to compose music (`Carscendo`), or video lag to pace human breathing (`Calmr`).
- **E — Eliminate:** Delete app installs (`Project OWL`), merchant onboarding (`ETHPark-QR`, `Ripe`), seed phrases (`Clave`), internet (`Xelio`), or per-bet popups (`Flownomo`).
- **R — Reverse / Inversion:** Flip the core premise 180° (`Ore`, `GibberLink`, `Split Finance`, `Dime Defender`, `Deltr`, `Exnight`, `Bulwark`).
